Services · one engagement path

Audit. Advise. Implement. Run.

Everything we do follows the same four steps, so a target or a legal requirement becomes a clear plan with costs and savings you can see. Buy a product, or just our people's time.

Book an audit Free energy check
1 · Entry point

Audit

Certified engineers check your energy and water use, onsite or remote, and show you where the savings are. The easiest place to start.

  • Electrical, chilled-water, compressed-air, boiler, water and pumping
  • How much of your equipment you really use, your backup plans and your sustainability gaps
  • Registered engineers for Singapore's mandatory energy-improvement filings
  • Customers usually cut energy costs by 15–35% within 12–18 months of acting on the audit

Fixed fee, USD 5k–25k

2 · Consulting & advisory

Advise

Clear plans with the numbers attached: what it costs to buy, what it costs to run, and what you get back.

  • Step-by-step plans to reach net-zero and energy certification
  • Help to earn green building ratings
  • Plans for sensors and upgrades in existing buildings
  • Market-entry advisory for manufacturers setting up in a new country: work permits, site visits and regulatory coordination

USD 200/hr or retainer

3 · Delivery

Implement

Non-intrusive installation over the infrastructure you already run.

  • Setting up our AI apps, including on your own servers
  • Connecting sensors and building controls
  • Upgrading data centres and air-con
  • Installing air-quality sensors, backup power and batteries, and checking the savings are real

Fixed price or hourly

4 · Managed services

Run · Cirtec Care

We stay with you after go-live, so the savings last.

  • Day-to-day technical support, and staff when you need them
  • Longer warranties and contracts that guarantee results
  • Paperwork for green building ratings
  • Annual sustainability-report authoring

Yearly fee or subscription
